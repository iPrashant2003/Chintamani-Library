RECITALS
A.  The Client owns and operates Chinta Mani Library, a multi-branch self-study library
business with branches at Khalilabad (72 Seats) and Mehdawal (65 Seats) in Sant Kabir
Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, and intends to expand to further branches.
B.  The Developer has independently engineered and developed a proprietary multi-branch
library management system comprising: (i) a cross-platform mobile application built with
Flutter/Dart (Android build) with an in-app Over-the-Air (OTA) update engine, a 4D
claymorphic interface, biometric and PIN authentication and offline caching; (ii) a
universal QR self-admission, student photo upload and attendance web portal built with
HTML5 and Tailwind, using a Cloudinary zero-copy multi-part photo pipeline; (iii) a cloud
backend and database architecture built with NestJS / Node.js, Neon PostgreSQL,
Render.com microservices and the Cloudinary CDN; and (iv) a dual-branch isolation
architecture providing strict compartmentalised data segregation between the Khalilabad
and Mehdawal branches, with scalable support for future branches (together, the
"Software").
C.  The Client wishes to obtain, and the Developer is willing to grant, a licence to deploy and
operate the Software for the Client's library business, and the Client wishes to engage the
Developer to maintain and support the Software on an ongoing basis.
D.  The Parties wish to record, in a single binding instrument, their respective rights in the
intellectual property, the Developer's right of attribution, the commercial terms, the
allocation of responsibility for third-party cloud services and personal data, and the terms
of maintenance, confidentiality, termination and dispute resolution.

and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties
agree as follows:
